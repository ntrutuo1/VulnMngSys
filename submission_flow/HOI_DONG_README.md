# VulnMngSys - Goi xem xet hoi dong

Goi nay duoc tao cho muc dich trinh bay va tham dinh luong xu ly cua he thong.

Day khong phai la goi cai dat va khong phai la goi ma nguon day du de chay lai ung dung. Cac file khoi dong, file cai dat phu thuoc, file build, file dependency, binary, cache va output da duoc loai bo co chu dich.

## Muc tieu cua goi

- Cho phep xem tong quan kien truc he thong.
- Cho phep theo doi flow xu ly tu giao dien/CLI den scanner, rule, scoring va report.
- Giu du thong tin de hoi dong hieu cach thiet ke va cach cac khoi lien ket voi nhau.
- Khong cung cap vat lieu de cai dat, build, reverse thanh ban chay, hoac tai lap moi truong thuc thi.

## Noi dung chinh

- `SOURCE_FLOW.md`: flow xu ly chuan cua he thong.
- `PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md`: vai tro cac thu muc/module.
- `FUNCTIONAL_SCOPE.md`: pham vi chuc nang va cac dich vu duoc ho tro.
- `PACKAGING_NOTE.md`: ghi chu ve cac thanh phan da loai bo.
- `README.md`: tong quan rut gon cho nguoi xem.

## Luu y

Goi nay chi nen dung de tham khao, bao ve va trinh bay. Neu can chay he thong, phai su dung bo ma nguon noi bo day du cua nhom phat trien.
